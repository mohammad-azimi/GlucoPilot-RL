GlucoPilot-RL — Phase 2 correction
===================================

This update fixes the baseline evaluation horizon and plot labels:
- simglucose reports a 3-minute sample interval in this environment.
- A full 24-hour experiment therefore uses 480 steps, not 288.
- Meal markers are now positioned on the correct simulated-hour axis.
- The simulator seed is passed at environment construction for fair action comparison.
- The candidate action range is expanded because the first dry-run selected its upper boundary.
- Baseline selection is safety-first before mean-risk ranking.

Copy the project files into the existing GlucoPilot-RL directory, keep the
existing .venv folder, and run:

    .venv\Scripts\activate
    python scripts\evaluate_fixed_basal.py
